02 / THE DECISIONS

Nothing here by accident.

The original build rationale, from the glass to the headers.

DISPLAY & ENCLOSURE

The screen is the case.

The Thermalright TR-A70 Vision White puts a 9.16 inch, 1920 × 480 display along the top. Glass on the front, side and top makes the internal choices part of the exterior.

Inspect enclosure ↗
LIGHT & AIR

Five fans. Three with RGB.

Three bottom fans pull air in. Two plain white rear fans push hot air out. Lighting stays on the glass strip, bottom fans and cooler — not on every component.

Inspect cooling ↗
CONNECTIONS & CLEARANCE

The small details matter.

The board’s three ARGB headers cover the case, cooler and fan hub. Two USB 2.0 headers run the two displays. The 151 mm cooler sits within the case’s 168.4 mm limit.

THE BUDGET REALITY

Why $1,533.49,
not $1,100.

The request and the parts list are not the same number. The original build records the white graphics card at $539.99, 16 GB of DDR5 at $239.99 and a 1 TB SSD at $156.00.

Original pricing context

Parts cost far more in 2026. Memory makers are moving production to AI server memory, so DDR5 and SSDs are priced well above normal.
